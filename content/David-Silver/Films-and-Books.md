*← [David Silver](David-Silver/) · [Silver Family Archive](./)*

Every film, book, play and broadcast that **David Silver** names in his letters home, from his first term at King's School, Bruton, in 1937, through the RAF (1943–47), to the end of his architecture training in Bristol in 1953. Each entry says whether he saw, read or heard it, or only mentions it, and gives the letter where it comes up. His own words are in quotation marks.

**44 films, 48 books and 21 plays, ballets and broadcasts.**

**Links.** Film titles link to IMDb (the IMDb id comes from Wikidata, matched on title and year; a few go to an IMDb search). Book titles link to Open Library. Plays and broadcasts link to Wikipedia where there is an article. Titles are given in their usual published form: David's spellings and slips ("Thatched Cottage", "Mr Pym passes By") are kept in the quotations.

## Films

### At school: King's School, Bruton (1937–1942)

- **[*Pinocchio*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0032910/)** (1940, dir. Ben Sharpsteen and Hamilton Luske, for Disney). Mentioned: [Bruton 54](David-Silver/Bruton-Letters#54-sunday-3-november-1940) (Sunday 3 November 1940). “The father of Pinocchio was Geppetto not Guiseppo.”
- **[*Man Hunt*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0033873/)** (1941, dir. Fritz Lang). Wanted to see it: [Bruton 57](David-Silver/Bruton-Letters#57-a-tuesday-october-1941) (A Tuesday, October 1941). “I want to see ‘Man Hunt’ very much.”
- **[*Quiet Wedding*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0032961/)** (1941, dir. Anthony Asquith). Missed it: [Bruton 57](David-Silver/Bruton-Letters#57-a-tuesday-october-1941) (A Tuesday, October 1941). “We evidently missed a very good film in ‘Quiet Wedding’ that came to the Savoy … It is supposed to have been absolutely screaming.”

Wanted to see it: [Skegness 04](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Skegness-Letters#04-monday-18--sunday-24-october-1943) (Monday 18 – Sunday 24 October 1943). “I am going to have a shot at going to ‘Lost Horizon’ next week. Saw it: [Skegness 15](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Skegness-Letters#15-christmas-day-1943--sunday-26-december) (Christmas Day 1943 – Sunday 26 December). “a British film and though a little disjointed makes a very nice escape from the war.”

Saw it: [Skegness 16](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Skegness-Letters#16-sunday-2-january-1944-his-twentieth-birthday) (Sunday 2 January 1944: his twentieth birthday). - **[*The Song of Bernadette*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0036377/)** (1943, dir. Henry King). Saw it: [Oulton 01](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#01-saturday-c-17-june-1944-just-arrived-at-oulton) (Saturday, c. 17 June 1944). - **[*The Young Mr. Pitt*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0035586/)** (1942, dir. Carol Reed). Saw it: [Oulton 03](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#03-thursday-to-wednesday-july-1944), [Oulton 06](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#06-a-monday-c-mid-september-1944), [Oulton 08](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#08-saturday-21-october-1944) (Thursday to Wednesday, July 1944). An ENSA showing at Oulton: “the film broke down five or six times and the sound track was very poor.”
- **[*This Happy Breed*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0037367/)** (1944, dir. David Lean). Saw it: [Oulton 06](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#06-a-monday-c-mid-september-1944) (A Monday, c. mid-September 1944). “I would recommend it very highly indeed … miles above any film I've seen for a long time.”
- **[*Gone with the Wind*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0031381/)** (1939, dir. Victor Fleming). Mentioned: [Oulton 06](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#06-a-monday-c-mid-september-1944) (A Monday, c. mid-September 1944). One of his yardsticks for a good film, with *The Young Mr Pitt* and *Pygmalion*.
- **[*Pygmalion*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0030637/)** (1938, dir. Anthony Asquith and Leslie Howard). Mentioned: [Oulton 06](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#06-a-monday-c-mid-september-1944) (A Monday, c. mid-September 1944). Another of his yardsticks for a good film.

Saw it: [Oulton 07](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#07-sunday-8--wednesday-11-october-1944) (Sunday 8 – Wednesday 11 October 1944). “Bing Crosby … is a Roman catholic priest and I thought it was quite good.”
- **[*Goodbye, Mr. Chips*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0031385/)** (1939, dir. Sam Wood). Saw it: [Oulton 08](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#08-saturday-21-october-1944) (Saturday 21 October 1944). Seen at Aylsham.
- **[*Rebecca*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0032976/)** (1940, dir. Alfred Hitchcock). Saw it: [Oulton 10](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#10-saturday-18--thursday-23-november-1944-back-from-leave) (Saturday 18 – Thursday 23 November 1944). In Norwich, “with Laurence Olivier and Joan Fontaine which is I believe an old film reissued.”
- **[*The Uninvited*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0037415/)** (1944, dir. Lewis Allen). Saw it: [Oulton 13](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#13-saturday-31-march--easter-sunday-1-april-1945) (Saturday 31 March – Easter Sunday 1 April 1945). “a ghost film and very good in its own way.”
- **[*Western Approaches*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0037455/)** (1944, dir. Pat Jackson). Saw it: [Oulton 13](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#13-saturday-31-march--easter-sunday-1-april-1945) (Saturday 31 March – Easter Sunday 1 April 1945). “I had heard a good deal about ‘Western Approaches’ and found it, as I expected, excellent … in ‘restrained’ Technicolor and the sea looked lovely.”
- **[*Twilight Hour*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0037406/)** (1944, dir. Paul L. Stein). Saw it: [Oulton 13](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oulton-Letters#13-saturday-31-march--easter-sunday-1-april-1945) (Saturday 31 March – Easter Sunday 1 April 1945). The second feature with *Western Approaches* at the Regent, Norwich.

- **[*Caesar and Cleopatra*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0038390/)** (1945, dir. Gabriel Pascal). Saw it: [Oakington 05](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Oakington-Letters#05-friday-14-december-1945), [Isle of Man 12](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#12-saturday-4-january-1947-back-from-christmas-his-23rd-birthday) (Friday 14 December 1945). Saw it: [Henlow 02](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Henlow-Letters#02-saturday-15-june-1946) (Saturday 15 June 1946). “I saw Bette Davis in the Corn is Green … she acted very well but a bit like you would expect from the author Emlyn Williams - a bit Welsh and depressing.”

Saw it: [Isle of Man 01](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#01-saturday--sunday-c-2728-july-1946-arrival-on-the-island) (Saturday – Sunday, c. 27–28 July 1946). His first evening in Ramsey: “I saw Waterloo Bridge (not Waterloo Road) it was old and not terribly good.”
- **[*I Know Where I'm Going!*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0037800/)** (1945, dir. Michael Powell and Emeric Pressburger). Saw it: [Isle of Man 05](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#05-a-tuesday-c-early-september-1946-the-races-german-prisoners) (A Tuesday, c. early September 1946). In the station cinema for fourpence, “a disused dining room and the film bounces up and down on the screen.”
- **[*O.S.S.*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0038794/)** (1946, dir. Irving Pichel). Saw it: [Isle of Man 06](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#06-saturday-14--wednesday-18-september-1946-moving-to-jurby-a-flight-in-a-wellington) (Saturday 14 – Wednesday 18 September 1946). “quite good but not nearly as exciting as apparently Philip thought … O.S.S. was rather Hollywood.”
- **[*The Last Chance*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0037871/)** (1945, dir. Leopold Lindtberg). Saw it: [Isle of Man 06](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#06-saturday-14--wednesday-18-september-1946-moving-to-jurby-a-flight-in-a-wellington) (Saturday 14 – Wednesday 18 September 1946). “If you want to see a film which justifies your being sick with excitement … see the Swiss film ‘The Last Chance’.”
- **[*Piccadilly Incident*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0038842/)** (1946, dir. Herbert Wilcox). Saw it: [Isle of Man 10](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#10-wednesday--thursday-c-early-december-1946-henry-swains-watercolour) (Wednesday – Thursday, c. early December 1946). “a British film and well worth seeing. - **[*Theirs Is the Glory*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0171824/)** (1946, dir. Brian Desmond Hurst). Saw it: [Isle of Man 10](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#10-wednesday--thursday-c-early-december-1946-henry-swains-watercolour) (Wednesday – Thursday, c. early December 1946). “the film about Arnhem”.
- **[*Great Expectations*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0038574/)** (1946, dir. David Lean). Saw it: [Isle of Man 12](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#12-saturday-4-january-1947-back-from-christmas-his-23rd-birthday) (Saturday 4 January 1947). - **[*School for Secrets*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0038911/)** (1946, dir. Peter Ustinov). Saw it: [Isle of Man 14](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#14-sunday-26-january-1947-posted-to-wellesbourne-then-not) (Sunday 26 January 1947). About radar: “all about the work I should have done if I hadn't been shoved onto the ground … one of the best films of I have seen.”

- **[*The Overlanders*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0038821/)** (1946, dir. Harry Watt). Mentioned: [Isle of Man 15](David-Silver/RAF-Letters/Isle-of-Man-Letters#15-c-mid-february-1947-back-from-leave) (c. mid-February 1947). Praised in a magazine he sent home.

### At architecture school in Bristol (1947–1953)

- **[*Life with Father*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0039566/)** (1947, dir. Michael Curtiz). Saw it: [Bristol 05](David-Silver/Bristol-Letters#05-a-sunday-c-28-november-1948-the-information-bureau) (A Sunday, c. 28 November 1948). “I didn't think it was bad but other people seem to have thought more of it than I did.”
