Places that run through the family story, in date order. Tresillian, the family house in Exeter, and Bedford Circus, where Clifford had his surgery, are covered on [Clifford Marking Silver's biography](Clifford-Marking-Silver/Biography#tresillian).

- **1st–3rd century AD** · [Romano-British Antiquities Found at Topsham](Denver-Excavation): finds from Park Field, opposite the Pyne family's Denver Nurseries, catalogued in 1934–35. Anthea's family, the Pynes, appear in [her memoir](Anthea-Silver/A-Topsham-Childhood#chapter-5-the-pyne-family).
- **1930s–1942** · [King's School, Bruton](Kings-School-Bruton): where Christopher and David boarded, and the boys David knew who are named on its Second World War memorial.
