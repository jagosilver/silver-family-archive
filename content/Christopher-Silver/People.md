*← [Christopher Patrick Silver](../)*

The People photographs form one of the most intriguing and frustrating parts of the collection. Twenty-three photographs survive showing individuals and groups — family members, friends, medical colleagues, and strangers on a promenade. The identities of most subjects are unknown. As Basil Macdonald noted when sending the scans: *"I'd really like to know who everybody is as I can only guess three or four people."*

Some tentative identifications are offered below, but all should be treated as provisional. If you recognise anyone, please add notes to the discussion page.

## Portraits — Christopher Silver
.

![Formal studio portrait: a young man with curly dark hair, in a suit and tie, seated and looking directly at the camera. The simplicity of the composition and the glass plate technique suggest this was taken by CPS himself on a self-timer, or by someone in the family. *Christopher Silver in his late teens or early twenties.*](../images/CPS-People-22.jpg)

![The same young man (or a very similar one) photographed outdoors, seated in a wicker chair in a garden, wearing the same style of grey suit. A stone wall is visible behind. The setting may be the [Silver family home in Exeter](Christopher-Silver/UK#Exeter). *IChristopher Silver.*](../images/CPS-People-23.jpg)

![A formal portrait of a young man with distinctive round tortoiseshell glasses, seated in front of a curtain with a leaded window behind — the setting suggests a college room or a well-furnished house. The glasses date the photograph to the late 1930s or early 1940s. *[David Silver](David-Silver), Jago's maternal grandfather.*](../images/CPS-People-17.jpg)

## Academic gown portrait

![A young man in an academic gown with fur-trimmed hood, shown in profile, resting one hand on a chair back. The gown suggests an Oxford Bachelor of Medicine degree — consistent with CPS qualifying at Hertford College, Oxford in 1942. *Possibly CPS at his Oxford graduation.*](../images/CPS-People-01.jpg)

## Young woman in the garden

![A young woman lying on a tartan picnic rug on a close-mown lawn, propped on her elbows, looking to her left — a summer dress with a bold pattern, her hair loose. A flower border and hedge are visible behind. The garden appears to be the [Exeter family home](Christopher-Silver/UK#Exeter). Tenderly and naturally composed. *Identity unknown — possibly Nancy Silver (née Pym), who later became CPS's wife, or a sister.*](../images/CPS-People-03.jpg)

## Family picnic

![Four people enjoying a clifftop or moorland picnic — two men and two women, informally dressed in 1930s fashion. A picnic basket and thermos flask sit between them; a 1930s motor car is parked in the background. A holiday atmosphere. *Identities unknown — possibly the Silver family on a Devon or Cornwall outing.*](../images/CPS-People-06.jpg)

## Two men with bones

![Two men on open moorland: a younger man watches as an older man in a felt hat and glasses examines a small animal skull or bone with evident interest. The older man has a camera strap visible. The mood is absorbed and natural — a naturalist's moment. *Identities unknown.*](../images/CPS-People-14.jpg)

## Seaside promenade

![Two men walking on a seaside promenade — a beach with bathers below, a steep white chalk or limestone headland across the bay. The taller man is younger; the shorter man uses a walking stick. A busy summer resort, possibly on the Devon coast. *Identities unknown.*](../images/CPS-People-11.jpg)

## Medical colleagues

The one group of photographs where identities are at least partially known. CPS was noted for using his photographic skills to document his medical teams.

![Three doctors in white coats photographed against a rendered wall — January 1968, according to the original slide label. Identified in the collection as **Dr Rahman**, **Dr John**, and **Dr Ahmed** — members of CPS's medical team, probably at one of his East London hospitals (St Matthew's, Bethnal Green, the London Mile End, or St Andrew's). A significant document of his clinical life.](../images/CPS-People-09.jpg)
